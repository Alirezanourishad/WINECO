# Experimental validation

Source: WIN E CO, Final Review (academic year 2025/2026), Interdisciplinary Projects, Politecnico di Torino.

## Test campaign

The physical prototype was observed in an office environment across four test days, 26–29 May 2026. The team reported 71 operating hours and 8.46 hours of recorded data gaps, mainly due to temporary power or system downtime.

## Results

| Metric | Reported result |
| --- | --- |
| Appropriate responses to recorded KPI violations | 96.215% |
| Positive feedback on KPI clarity | 80% |
| Positive feedback on dashboard visual clarity | 92.5% |
| Combined positive feedback on KPI visualization | 86.25% |
| Survey responses | 40 |
| Visual comfort band (400–2000 lux), zone 1 | 13.36% of available readings |
| Visual comfort band (400–2000 lux), zone 2 | 46.72% of available readings |

The survey measures perceived dashboard clarity, not environmental performance. Illuminance was logged when changes exceeded 50 lux rather than at a fixed interval; these percentages describe available readings, not continuous time in comfort.

## Limitations

- Thermal and visual comfort acceptance targets were not met.
- CO₂/IAQ data and communication results prevented full IAQ validation.
- The test campaign included downtime and data gaps.
- Control response is distinct from actual room-level environmental improvement.
- Quantified energy savings were not established by the campaign.

## Engineering lifecycle

Kick-off → System Requirements Review → Preliminary Design Review → Critical Design Review → Test Readiness Review → Final Review.

The results demonstrate the integration and operation of an IoT prototype while identifying constraints to address in future work. They do not establish production readiness.
